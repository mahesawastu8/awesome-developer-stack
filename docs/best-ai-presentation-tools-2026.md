## 2. The Core Problem with Standalone AI Slide Builders

Tools like Gamma and Tome are visually impressive when viewed inside their proprietary web viewers. They use fluid, card-based web layouts that look modern and sleek.

However, the moment you need to present to a client, executive board, or cross-functional team, reality hits:
1. **The Export Penalty:** When you export a Gamma or Tome deck to `.pptx` or Google Slides, typography alignments shift, grid columns break, and charts convert into non-editable raster images.
2. **The Collaboration Barrier:** Enterprise teams do not want another login. They collaborate in Google Slides and PowerPoint using version history, shared comments, and company permission hierarchies.
