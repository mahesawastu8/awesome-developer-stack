2. The Core Problem with Standalone AI Slide Builders

Tools like Gamma and Tome are visually impressive when viewed inside their proprietary web viewers. They use fluid, card-based web layouts that look modern and sleek.

However, the moment you need to present to a client, executive board, or cross-functional team, reality hits:

  • The Export Penalty: When you export a Gamma or Tome deck to `.pptx` or Google Slides, typography alignments shift, grid columns break, and charts convert into non-editable raster images.
  • The Collaboration Barrier: Enterprise teams do not want another login. They collaborate in Google Slides and PowerPoint using version history, shared comments, and company permission hierarchies.

    • 3. Why Plus AI is the Pragmatic Choice for Real Work

    Plus AI took the opposite engineering approach: instead of inventing a new presentation format, it operates directly as an approved add-on inside Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint.

    Key Workflow Advantages:

  • In-Situ Generation: You write your prompt or upload your research document, and Plus AI generates outlines, speaker notes, and multi-slide decks directly on your Google Slides canvas.
  • Preserves Master Templates: It respects your existing brand typography, color palettes, and slide layouts.

    • Single-Slide AI Rewriting: You don't have to regenerate the whole deck to fix one slide. You can ask Plus AI to "format this slide into a 3-column metric card" or "summarize these 5 bullet points into an executive takeaway"*.

    4. Final Recommendation

  • If you are a solo freelancer building an interactive web presentation: Gamma is worth exploring.
  • If you work in consulting, finance, tech, or any team that lives inside Google Workspace or Microsoft 365: Plus AI is the only tool that doesn't break enterprise workflows.
